## A. Project Overview
ForgeMind AI is an autonomous, high-performance manufacturing quality intelligence and decision-support platform. It bridges the critical divide between automated computer-vision defect detection, domain engineering knowledge (FMEA, SOPs, Root Cause Analysis), and historical factory resolution workflows.

ForgeMind AI operates as an **advisory decision-support system**. It assists manufacturing operators, quality managers, and reliability engineers by providing structured defect classification, explainability, failure mode hypotheses, historical precedent retrieval, and simulation-based economic assessment without claiming autonomous physical control over shop-floor machinery.

---

## B. Project Purpose & Operating Philosophy
1. **Advisory Decision-Support Only**: ForgeMind provides prioritized insights, procedural checklists, and historical context. It never issues direct physical actuation commands to CNC machines, conveyors, casting dies, or robotic transfer units without human verification.
2. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)**: All corrective decisions, SOP implementations, and status updates require human engineer confirmation (`[USER CONFIRMED]`).
3. **Strict Epistemic Decoupling**: Vision classifier certainty, visual similarity to past cases, and engineering causation are independent dimensions and are never conflated.
4. **Zero Fabrication Guarantee**: The system never generates fake financial losses, unmeasured machine parameters, non-existent station linkages, or artificial bounding boxes.

---

## C. Vision Model Architecture & Preprocessing
- **Base Architecture**: EfficientNet-B0 (PyTorch, convolutional neural network).
- **Pretraining**: Transfer learning initialized from ImageNet-1k weights.
- **Input Geometry**: 3-channel RGB image resized to `224 x 224` pixels.
- **Normalization**: Standard ImageNet mean `[0.485, 0.456, 0.406]` and standard deviation `[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]`.
- **Inference Pipeline**:
  ```
  Specimen Image 
  → OpenCV Quality Validation (blur, illumination, resolution) 
  → PyTorch Preprocessing (RGB convert, resize 224x224, normalize) 
  → EfficientNet-B0 Feature Extractor + Linear Classifier Head 
  → Softmax Probability Distribution & Top-1 Class 
  → Grad-CAM Gradient-Weighted Class Activation Mapping
  ```
- **Active Checkpoint**: `models/efficientnet_b0_forgemind_best.pth` (16.3 MB).

## D. Defect Classes
ForgeMind AI classifies metallic manufacturing specimens into exactly five mutually exclusive classes:
1. **Crack**: Linear mechanical fractures or thermal stress separations on the substrate surface.
2. **Hole**: Surface voids, gas entrainment pockets, or porosity cavities.
3. **Rust**: Ferrous oxidation patches, surface corrosion, or coolant chemical staining.
4. **Scratch**: Linear mechanical abrasions, tooling gouges, or conveyor rail rubbing marks.
5. **Normal**: Defect-free surface within acceptable engineering and aesthetic tolerances.

## F. Confidence Interpretation & Metric Decoupling
- **What Confidence Is**: The model confidence score (e.g., `99.5% [MODEL]`) is the mathematical softmax probability generated by EfficientNet-B0 over the 224x224 pixel input. It reflects visual feature certainty that the specimen matches the learned image pattern.
- **What Confidence Is NOT**:
  - It is **NOT** a root-cause probability.
  - It is **NOT** a similarity score to historical maintenance records.
  - It is **NOT** a physical severity rating.
- **Strict Decoupling Principle**: High visual confidence (e.g. 99.5%) does not mean the defect is severe or that a previous case caused it; it merely confirms the neural network is confident in the visual classification.

## G. Grad-CAM Explainability & Localization Limits
- **Technique**: Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) computed from the final convolutional layer of EfficientNet-B0 (`features[8]`).
- **Function**: Produces a 2D coarse heat map highlighting regions of the input image that contributed most strongly to the predicted class.
- **Strict Boundary Guarantee**:
  - Grad-CAM represents an **approximate attention region** only.
  - It is **NEVER** described as an exact bounding box, segmentation mask, or coordinate localization.
  - The system **never fabricates coordinate tuples** `[x1, y1, x2, y2]` or millimeter dimensional measurements from Grad-CAM heatmaps.
  - If exact coordinate annotation becomes available in future factory datasets, it will be introduced as a separate, distinct measured layer.

## H. Engineering Investigation Workflow
When a defect is detected:
1. **Retrieval**: The system queries the engineering knowledge base (`data/engineering_knowledge/`) for technical Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) documentation matching the defect class.
2. **Hypothesis Formulation**: Potential contributing factors are structured as engineering hypotheses (`[HYPOTHESIS]`), explicitly highlighting that physical on-site inspection is required for confirmation.
3. **Recommended Actions**: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and physical inspection checks are generated with `[ADVISORY]` tags.
4. **Non-Causality Rule**: Explanations use probabilistic, advisory phrasing (*"Potential contributing cause"*, *"Evidence suggests"*, *"May be associated with"*). Never state *"Confirmed root cause"* unless physical verification records prove it.

---

## I. Cure & Prevention Workflow
The **Cure & Prevention** module replaces passive chatbots with an active historical learning loop:
1. **Detect**: Current defect and visual confidence are displayed.
2. **Retrieve**: The system searches historical cases for visually and semantically similar records.
3. **Review**: The engineer inspects previous investigations, recommended SOPs, human decisions, and recorded outcomes.
4. **Apply Guidance**: The engineer can click `[ USE PREVIOUS ACTION ]` to adopt the historical procedure. This transitions the case to `ACTION_APPROVED [USER CONFIRMED]` with the explicit reminder that physical execution must be verified.
5. **Verify**: Once physical work is completed, the engineer marks the case `VERIFIED`.
6. **Learn**: The newly verified case is indexed into the historical case library for future retrieval.

## J. Historical Case Workflow
- **Data Structure**:
  - `case_id`: Unique identifier (e.g., `CASE-DEMO-018`, `CASE-HIST-FM-7714`).
  - `inspection_id`: Linked inspection run ID.
  - `defect_type`: Visual defect category.
  - `similarity_score`: Feature/attribute similarity percentage (`[SIMILARITY]`).
  - `previous_investigation`: Investigation procedure executed.
  - `possible_causes`: Factors considered (`[HYPOTHESIS]`).
  - `recommended_action`: Action advised (`[ADVISORY]`).
  - `selected_action`: Human-selected procedure (`[USER CONFIRMED]`).
  - `outcome`: Resolution status (`[USER CONFIRMED]`).
  - `verification_status`: `UNVERIFIED`, `VERIFIED`, or `DEFECT_RECURRED`.
- **Similarity Disclaimer**: Similarity indicates resemblance to a past specimen; it does **not** confirm that the current defect shares the same root cause.

## K. Human Approval Workflow (State Machine)
ForgeMind enforces an 8-state strict lifecycle:
```
[1. NEW]
    ↓
[2. INVESTIGATING]
    ↓
[3. ACTION_RECOMMENDED]  ←→  [REJECTED (Returns to INVESTIGATING)]
    ↓
[4. ACTION_APPROVED [USER CONFIRMED]]
    ↓
[5. ACTION_IN_PROGRESS]
    ↓
[6. RESOLVED]
    ↓
[7. VERIFICATION_PENDING]
    ↓
[8. VERIFIED [USER CONFIRMED]]  or  [DEFECT_RECURRED]
```
**Rule**: AI never silently transitions a case to `ACTION_APPROVED` or `VERIFIED`. Every status change requires explicit user confirmation.

## L. Economic Impact Simulation
1. **No Fabricated Financials**: Visual images do not contain scrap valuation, raw material pricing, labor hourly rates, or ERP billing numbers. ForgeMind **never invents rupee (₹), dollar ($), or euro (€) losses**.
2. **User-Selected Simulation Tiers**:
   - **LOW**: `5%` simulated demo impact score.
   - **MEDIUM**: `10%` simulated demo impact score.
   - **HIGH**: `20%` simulated demo impact score.
   - **Not Assessed**: `0%` reset state.
3. **Mandatory Disclosure**: All economic outputs are labeled `[SIMULATED]` with the disclosure: *"Demo impact score only. The supplied dataset does not contain product cost, criticality, or ERP records. This value is not an actual monetary loss."*

## M. Process Impact Analysis (Simulation Dataset)
- **Source**: Discrete-event production simulation dataset (Rockwell Arena, Mendeley DOI: 10.17632/3rw227zxt7.2).
- **Metrics Calculated**: Station utilization (`%`), line throughput (`units/hr`), queue waiting times (`seconds`), and bottleneck scores.
- **Strict Decoupling Rule**: The visual defect dataset (10,726 images) and production simulation dataset (Model 1 / Model 2) are **independent**. Visual defects are not physically linked to Arena machine records. Any analytical mapping is deterministic and explicitly labeled `[SIMULATED]`.

## N. What-If Simulation & Alternative Scenarios
The What-If Simulation module enables comparative assessment between baseline operations and alternative remediation scenarios:
- **Parameters Compared**:
  - Baseline vs Alternative Throughput (`units/hour`).
  - Baseline vs Alternative Cycle Time (`seconds`).
  - Baseline vs Alternative Defect Rate (`%`).
  - Simulated Impact Tier & Potential Relative Savings (`%`).
- **Provenance**: All hypothetical scenario outcomes are strictly tagged `[SIMULATED]`. They are mathematical projections for decision trade-off analysis, not guaranteed physical shop-floor outcomes.

## O. Multi-Dimensional Risk Assessment
When sufficient operational data is available, risk is evaluated across seven dimensions:
1. `defect_type`: Visual severity and structural criticality.
2. `product_criticality`: Operational role of the affected component.
3. `safety_relevance`: Whether defect impacts operator or end-user safety.
4. `quality_specification`: Tolerance threshold compliance.
5. `recurrence_rate`: Historical frequency of defect on this line.
6. `process_impact`: Downstream bottleneck propagation risk.
7. `reworkability`: Feasibility and cost of physical rectification.

**Incomplete Data Guardrail**: If product specification or batch records are missing, the system outputs: *"Risk assessment incomplete — required process/product data unavailable."* It never invents arbitrary risk scores.

## Q. Factory Assistant Capabilities

### Category A: Normal Manufacturing Conversation
- Friendly greetings and system capability explanations.
- Manufacturing definitions: CNC machining, EDM, throughput, cycle time, takt time, OEE.

  `inspection_id`, `defect_class`, `confidence`, `gradcam_available`, `case_status`, `recommended_actions`.
- Explains why a defect was classified, why a cause is labeled `[HYPOTHESIS]`, what similar historical cases exist, and what precautions to take before approving corrective actions.

### Anti-Hallucination Machine Constraint:
- If a user asks for specific unverified machine parameters (e.g., *"What spindle RPM should I run on Lathe 3?"* or *"What hydraulic clamping pressure is required?"*), the assistant strictly responds:
  *"I don't have verified information about that machine parameter in the available knowledge base."*
- It never invents RPM, feed rates, temperatures, bar pressures, or tolerances.

## R. System Limitations & Non-Claims
1. **No Direct Physical Actuation**: ForgeMind does not control PLCs, CNC controllers, or emergency e-stops.
2. **Occurrence Time Unavailable**: An image alone cannot reveal when a scratch or crack was introduced during production. ForgeMind states: *"Occurrence time: Not available from image data."*
3. **No Automatic Bounding Box**: Grad-CAM shows coarse attention gradients, not bounding coordinates.
4. **No Direct Financial Bookkeeping**: Currency values require ERP/MES integration that is not present in the organizer dataset.

## S. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: Does ForgeMind automatically repair machine defects?**  
*A: No. ForgeMind is strictly an advisory decision-support system. It provides historical guidance and SOP recommendations for human technicians to review, execute, and verify.*

**Q2: Is the vision model retrained after every user interaction?**  
*A: No. User interactions and verified cases update the RAG knowledge and historical case library, NOT the weights of the EfficientNet-B0 vision model.*

**Q3: Can high confidence guarantee that a defect caused a line stoppage?**  
*A: No. Vision confidence is an independent metric of classifier certainty over image pixels. It does not measure physical causation or shop-floor line stoppages.*

**Q4: Why does the economic impact score only show 5%, 10%, or 20%?**  
*A: Because the dataset contains no pricing, material scrap, or customer warranty data. These percentages are standardized user-selected simulation tiers to demonstrate analytical workflows.*

**Q5: What should I do if a defect recurs after marking a case verified?**  
*A: Transition the case status to `DEFECT_RECURRED`. This flags the specimen for full multi-factor engineering re-investigation.*

## T. Terminology Index
- **HITL (Human-in-the-Loop)**: Operational paradigm where AI recommendations must be confirmed by human authority.
- **FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis)**: Systematic method for evaluating process failure mechanisms.
- **SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)**: Established step-by-step guidance for quality and maintenance tasks.
- **Grad-CAM**: Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping for coarse convolutional explainability.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: Grounding LLM responses in verified external technical documents.

## U. Strict Data Provenance Rules
Every data element displayed by ForgeMind AI must carry an explicit epistemic badge:
- `[MEASURED]`: Direct physical measurement or vision classifier output over real specimen pixels.
- `[CALCULATED]`: Mathematically derived from verified datasets (e.g., Arena throughput or utilization).
- `[ESTIMATED]`: Statistically approximated values with stated confidence margins.
- `[SIMULATED]`: Generated by Rockwell Arena simulation or user-selected economic what-if scenarios.
- `[HYPOTHESIS]`: Potential engineering contributing factor derived from FMEA references; unconfirmed on specimen.
- `[ADVISORY]`: Procedural recommendation requiring human validation before execution.
- `[USER INPUT]`: Data or notes provided by the human operator.
- `[USER CONFIRMED]`: Approval or verification status formally signed off by human engineer.
- `[HISTORICAL EVIDENCE]`: Prior maintenance actions or recorded outcomes from previously verified cases.
- `[DETERMINISTIC FALLBACK]`: Rule-based engineering guidance used when external generative services are offline.
