## Engineering Context & Failure Mechanisms
Surface rust on exposed ferrous surfaces occurs when aqueous moisture and ambient oxygen breach the protective boundary layer:
1. **Coolant Emulsion Degradation:** Depletion of water-soluble anti-corrosion additives or drop in emulsion pH below 8.8 reduces the oil passivation barrier on freshly machined surfaces.
2. **Post-Machining Wash & Drying Delay:** Inadequate air knife blower velocity or temperature allows aqueous rinse water to pool on component surfaces.
3. **Staging Ambient Humidity:** Prolonged staging in non-climate-controlled storage areas accelerates galvanic/atmospheric oxidation.

## Advisory Investigation Steps
- Titrate cutting coolant emulsion concentration and verify pH remains within 8.8–9.2.
- Inspect wash stage drying chamber air temperature and blower velocity.
- Review intermediate staging transfer dwell times between machining and packaging.
