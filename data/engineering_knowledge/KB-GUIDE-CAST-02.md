## Engineering Context & Failure Mechanisms
Internal and surface porosity voids (holes) in cast or sintered metals typically stem from gas precipitation or turbulent entrapment:
1. **Entrapped Gas & Venting Restrictions:** Air or decomposed mold release lubricant becomes trapped when die cavity venting channels become partially restricted.
2. **Crucible Degassing Inadequacy:** Dissolved hydrogen gas precipitating during solidification creates spherical porosity voids.
3. **Plunger Injection Velocity:** High shot sleeve velocity causing turbulent fluid front rather than laminar filling.

## Advisory Investigation Steps
- Inspect die cavity vacuum venting channels and air purge valves.
- Verify crucible inert gas (argon/nitrogen) degassing cycle duration.
- Audit shot plunger velocity profile during cavity fill stage.
