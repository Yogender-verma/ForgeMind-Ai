## Engineering Context & Failure Mechanisms
Components exhibiting baseline surface finish with no macroscopic cracks, voids, abrasions, or corrosion satisfy standard quality acceptance criteria:
1. **Compliant Surface Integrity:** Uniform metallurgical texture within nominal surface roughness thresholds.
2. **Process Stability:** Upstream machining, cooling, and handling cycles operated within controlled variance bounds.

## Advisory Investigation Steps
- Maintain standard preventative maintenance cadence on tooling and fluid systems.
- Log inspection verification record in quality audit registry.
