## Engineering Context & Failure Mechanisms
Linear scratches and surface abrasions originate from mechanical friction or particulate contamination during transfer and clamping:
1. **Metallic Swarf Accumulation:** Chips or machining debris embedded on fixture resting pads score the workpiece under clamping pressure.
2. **Transfer Gripper Jaw Wear:** Misaligned or worn robotic end-effector fingers cause rubbing abrasions during load/unload cycles.
3. **Conveyor Guide Rail Friction:** Direct contact with unpadded metal guide rails during transfer between work centers.

## Advisory Investigation Steps
- Inspect automated air-blow swarf evacuation nozzles on fixture seating pads.
- Check transfer gripper finger rubber pads and alignment tolerances.
- Inspect conveyor transfer guide rails for burrs or particulate buildup.
